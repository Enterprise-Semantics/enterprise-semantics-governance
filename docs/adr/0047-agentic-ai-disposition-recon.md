# ADR-ES-038 ; Agentic AI Semantic Disposition Recon

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-28
Deciders: eaojnr
Semantic Area: Agentic Behavior, Artificial Intelligence
Candidate: Agentic AI
Predecessors: ES-ADR-004, ES-ADR-022, ES-ADR-037
Target: Disposition decision, not automatic canonicalization

## 1. Decision

The recon SHALL determine whether the candidate represents a semantic concept, a characteristic/profile, an implementation pattern, a technology-dependent realization, or terminology that should remain non-canonical.

Expected disposition: Technology/capability characteristic or external terminology. SHALL NOT introduce Agentic AI merely to complete Agentic/AI symmetry.

## 2. Required Recon Questions

The recon SHALL establish: Does Agentic AI represent a semantic concept, an AI technology characteristic, a capability profile, an implementation architecture, or external terminology ?

It SHALL examine whether the candidate provides semantic information not already captured through the existing canonical taxonomy (Agent, Agentic, Autonomous, Closed Loop, Operations, etc.).

## 3. Negative Boundary Tests

The recon SHALL reject equivalences:

- Agentic AI != Agentic
- Agentic AI != AI Agent
- Agentic AI != Autonomous AI
- Agentic AI != Autonomous

## 4. Disposition

Candidate disposition: Technology/capability characteristic or external terminology. SHALL NOT introduce Agentic AI merely to complete Agentic/AI symmetry.

Canonicalization requires evidence that the term provides semantic information not already captured through the existing taxonomy.
